# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in contributing to this project!

This project is developed and maintained by the Cardano High Assurance team, an engineering team within IOG. We welcome community contributions that help us identify opportunities to improve and maintain the extension.

For now, there are three primary ways to contribute:

* **Report bugs** — Let us know when something isn't working as expected.
* **Suggest improvements** — Share ideas for improving the functionality, usability, or overall experience of the extension.
* **Improve documentation** — Let us know when documentation is incorrect, unclear, incomplete, or could otherwise be improved.

Community contributions are currently managed through GitHub Issues. The Cardano High Assurance team will review and triage submitted issues and determine appropriate next steps.

---

# Contribution Process

The current contribution process is:

**Identify → Submit → Triage → Plan → Develop**

## 1. Identify an Issue or Improvement

Community members can contribute by identifying something that could improve the extension or its documentation.

This may include:

* a bug or unexpected behavior;
* an idea for improving existing functionality;
* an idea for new functionality; or
* incorrect, unclear, or missing documentation.

Before creating a new issue, please check the existing GitHub Issues to see whether the problem or idea has already been reported.

If an existing issue covers the same topic, you are encouraged to add relevant information or feedback to that issue rather than creating a duplicate.

---

## 2. Submit a GitHub Issue

Create a GitHub Issue describing the bug, improvement, idea, or documentation change.

Please provide enough information for the development team to understand the issue and why it may be valuable to address.

Depending on the type of issue, this may include:

### Bugs

* What happened?
* What did you expect to happen?
* How can the problem be reproduced?
* What version of the extension and VS Code are you using?
* Are there relevant screenshots, logs, or error messages?

Please remove sensitive or private information before submitting logs or screenshots.

### Improvements and New Ideas

* What would you like to see changed or added?
* What problem would this solve?
* How would the improvement help users?
* Are there examples or additional context that would help explain the idea?

### Documentation

* What documentation needs to be changed?
* What is incorrect, unclear, or missing?
* What information would make the documentation more useful?

---

## 3. Triage

The Cardano High Assurance team will periodically review submitted issues.

During triage, the team may:

* ask for additional information;
* investigate the reported problem;
* clarify the proposed improvement;
* identify duplicate or related issues;
* determine whether the issue should be addressed; or
* determine whether the issue should be considered for future development.

Submitting an issue does not guarantee that the proposed change will be implemented.

The team may consider factors such as:

* impact on users;
* alignment with the purpose of the extension;
* technical feasibility;
* compatibility with existing functionality;
* implementation effort;
* maintenance requirements; and
* available development capacity.

When appropriate, the issue will be updated to communicate its status or next steps.

---

## 4. Planning

Issues selected for development will be further investigated and defined by the Cardano High Assurance team.

This may include:

* clarifying requirements;
* defining expected behavior;
* designing the user experience;
* determining the technical approach;
* establishing acceptance criteria; and
* planning the work for development.

Community members may be asked for additional information or feedback when it would help the team better understand the problem or proposed improvement.

---

## 5. Development

Once an issue has been sufficiently defined and prioritized, the Cardano High Assurance team may incorporate it into future development work.

The issue can then be used to communicate relevant development progress and ultimately document when the change has been completed.

# Questions and Discussion

We encourage community members to participate by sharing problems, ideas, and feedback through GitHub Issues.
